YandexBackup

YandexBackup is a personal backup application used by its owner to create and maintain copies of selected Yandex Disk files in the owner's Google Drive.

The backup workflow runs on the owner's Mac. Working files remain at their original paths. Backup copies are additive: removing an original does not automatically remove its stored copy, and changed backup files retain their previous versions.

The Google connection requests the drive.file permission for application-created or explicitly authorized files. Existing Google Drive content is handled separately to preserve the owner's organization and sharing settings.

This application has no public registration, paid service, public document gallery or advertising. Publishing this information page does not publish any backup files.
