Use information

Effective date: 20 October 2026.

YandexBackup is a personal utility for its owner's authorized accounts. These pages describe the utility; they do not offer a public backup service, create an account or grant access to any Google Drive files.

The owner decides which files may be copied, where copies are stored and which access permissions are granted. Only content the owner is authorized to access and copy should be included.

Operating the utility requires the owner's Mac, network connectivity, valid service authorization and sufficient storage. A completed local copy is not proof of a complete cloud backup; cloud upload and source coverage require separate verification.

The utility is designed to preserve source files and retained backup versions. Backup files should not be used as the authoritative working copies. The owner may revoke authorization through the connected account's controls.
